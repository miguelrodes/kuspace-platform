# Recruiter Demo Walkthrough

This path is designed for a focused 90–120 second product demonstration using the fictional Neon Harbor workspace.

1. Open **Office** and identify the populated May 2030 calendar, the live Lumen Assembly event, and the upcoming, draft, past, and cancelled event states.
2. Open **Lumen Assembly** and move through Cover, Lineup, and Timetable to show multi-room programming and room-aware scheduling.
3. Open Guestlist and Budget to show access groups, check-in state, operational attendees, categorized expenses, and ticket-revenue inputs.
4. Open Tickets to show public and restricted sections, multiple price phases, release modes, sold inventory, and remaining capacity.
5. Return to Office, open **Low Tide Circuit**, and show the pending curated application with its recruiter-controlled access-group approval and denial actions.
6. Open the published recruiter/public event view to show how the configured event aggregate becomes the consumer-facing listing.
7. Use **Attendees** from an event editor to show paid attendee rows, access state, ticket-phase sales, check-in state, inventory, and revenue derived from seeded orders.

Avoid using Stripe onboarding or Checkout in the walkthrough until a dedicated test-mode deployment and signed webhook round trip have been verified.
